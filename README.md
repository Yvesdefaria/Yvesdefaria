### 🚀 &nbsp;Featured Projects
![-----------------------------------------------------](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/andreasbm/readme/master/assets/lines/aqua.png)

**🏋️ Gym Lab** · Fitness training and tracking app\
Local-first app (web + mobile via Capacitor) that works 100% offline: custom routines, set/rep/weight logging, rest timers, progress charts, PR tracking, progress photos and fitness calculators. Layered architecture (pure domain layer + repositories over IndexedDB), light/dark theme, ES/EN i18n, WCAG accessibility, and TDD with Vitest and Playwright.\
`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `Dexie` `Zustand` `Recharts` `Capacitor` `PWA`

**🧴 For-Him** · E-commerce for men's grooming products (team of 6 · QA Lead)\
Front-end in SvelteKit (Svelte 5 runes) connected to a PHP + MySQL REST API: auth flow, product listing and detail, cart, favorites and admin CRUD. I designed the Figma prototype and led the functional QA plan.\
`SvelteKit` `JavaScript` `PHP` `MySQL` `Figma` `Git/GitHub`
